====================================================================
  THE GIFT CURATOR — PRODUCT IMAGES FOLDER
====================================================================

You can easily add or replace product photos here!

HOW TO ADD A PHOTO FOR A PRODUCT:
1. Take or save your photo (JPG, PNG, or WebP).
2. Save it directly into this folder:
   C:\didi website\PRODUCT_IMAGES\
   (and in: C:\didi website\frontend\public\images\products\)

3. Recommended image filenames:
   - product-01.jpg -> The Velvet Morning Hamper
   - product-02.jpg -> Botanical Serenity & Tea Ritual
   - product-03.jpg -> The Executive Desk Suite
   - product-04.jpg -> Sweet Beginnings: Mum & Baby
   - product-05.jpg -> Artisan Host & Housewarming
   - product-06.jpg -> Golden Hour: Scent & Sweet
   - product-07.jpg -> Gentleman's Grooming & Amber
   - product-08.jpg -> Midnight Cocoa & Truffle Treat

HOW TO LINK A NEW PHOTO TO A PRODUCT:
- Open: C:\didi website\frontend\src\data\products.json
- Find the product and set:
  "image": "/images/products/product-01.jpg"

IF NO PHOTO IS ADDED YET:
- The website automatically shows an elegant boutique placeholder frame 
  with the hamper's name, so the layout, columns, and cards always look 
  clean and beautiful!
